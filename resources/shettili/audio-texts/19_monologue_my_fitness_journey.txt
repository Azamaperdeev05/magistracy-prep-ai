Speaker 1:
A little over two years ago, I was at the heaviest I'd ever been. I weighed almost one hundred kilograms, and I felt tired all the time. Just walking up a flight of stairs would leave me completely out of breath. I knew I needed to make a change, but I didn't know where to start. One morning, I saw a photo of myself from a family gathering, and I barely recognized the person in it. That was the moment I decided something had to give.

I didn't join a gym right away. Honestly, I was too embarrassed. Instead, I started walking. Just thirty minutes every evening around my neighborhood. At first, it was hard, but after a week, it started to feel normal. Then I added some light jogging. Then I looked up beginner workout videos on my phone and started doing them in my living room.

The hardest part was the diet. I had to completely change the way I thought about food. I used to eat a lot of processed snacks and sugary drinks. Slowly, I replaced them with fruits, vegetables, and lean proteins. I didn't go on some extreme diet — I just made small, sustainable changes. I started cooking at home more, and I learned to actually enjoy preparing healthy meals.

After about three months, I had lost around ten kilograms. That gave me the confidence to join a gym. I was nervous at first, but I quickly realized that most people are too focused on their own workouts to pay attention to anyone else. I started lifting weights, and I fell in love with it. There's something incredibly satisfying about getting a little bit stronger every week.

Now, about two and a half years later, I've lost over thirty kilograms. But more importantly, I feel amazing. I have energy, I sleep better, and I'm mentally sharper. Exercise has become a part of my daily routine, not a chore. If you're thinking about starting your own fitness journey, my advice is simple: just start. Don't wait for the perfect plan. Walk for ten minutes today. Cook one healthy meal. The small steps add up, I promise.
