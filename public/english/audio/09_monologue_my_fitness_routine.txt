I used to hate waking up early. But now, my morning workout is honestly my favorite part of the day. Let me walk you through my routine.

I wake up at six. The first thing I do is drink a full glass of water. Then I put on my workout clothes and head to the home gym I set up in my garage. It's nothing fancy. Just a bench, some dumbbells, a yoga mat, and a pull-up bar.

I start with a five-minute warm-up. Jumping jacks, arm circles, and leg swings. Getting the blood flowing is essential. If I skip the warm-up, my muscles feel stiff for the rest of the day.

Then I move into strength training. I follow a simple split. Monday and Thursday are upper body. Tuesday and Friday are legs and core. Wednesday and Saturday are full body, lighter weights but more reps. Sunday is my rest day.

For upper body, I do three sets of push-ups, dumbbell rows, overhead presses, and pull-ups. For legs, I focus on squats, lunges, and deadlifts. I finish every workout with a two-minute plank and some stretching.

After the strength work, I do twenty minutes of cardio. Usually I jump rope or go for a quick run around the neighborhood. It gets my heart rate up and clears my mind.

The whole thing takes about forty-five minutes. By the time I'm done, I feel energized and ready to take on whatever the day throws at me.

The best part? I never regret a workout. Even on days when I don't feel like it, I tell myself to just do the warm-up. Nine times out of ten, I end up finishing the whole routine. Starting is the hardest part. Once you start, the momentum carries you through.
